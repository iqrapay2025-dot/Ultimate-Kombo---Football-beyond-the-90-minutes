# Figma Make prompt: Ultimate Kombo, "Football Beyond the 90 Minutes"

Copy everything below the line into Figma Make.

---

## Goal

Design a responsive, single-page football media hub for **Ultimate Kombo** (TikTok: @ultimatekombo2, brand line: "Football From My Eyes"). It is a pitch concept for a football creator who covers Barcelona, the Premier League, the Champions League, transfers, tactics and opinions. The page should feel like a real football media platform, not a TikTok portfolio and not a Barça fan site.

The message of the page: "I used your love for Barça to design the visual identity, but built the platform around your entire football voice."

## Visual identity

Borrow the Barcelona visual language without using any crests, logos or club branding.

- **Colours:** Blaugrana blue `#004D98`, garnet red `#A50044`, gold accent `#EDBB00`, warm cream `#F6F0E4`, deep navy `#08142B`.
- **Themes:** light (cream background, navy text) as the default, plus a dark navy variant. Both must be readable and consistent.
- **Typography:** bold condensed display type for headlines (Big Shoulders Display or similar), a clean humanist sans for body text (Barlow or similar), and one handwritten script font used once for a hero annotation (Caveat or similar).
- **Graphic devices:** subtle vertical blue stripes behind the hero, half-blue / half-garnet colour blocks, pitch-line graphics, gold play buttons.
- **Photography:** editorial football style. Use placeholder frames where photos go, clearly marked "portrait" or "match photo". Do not use real player likenesses or club crests.
- **Shape language:** square corners on media blocks, thin borders, generous spacing, strong hierarchy. Avoid soft shadows and generic rounded SaaS cards.

## Behaviour and prototype notes

- Sticky navigation with anchor links to each section.
- Prototype these interactions: fixtures tab switch, tactical phase switch with player movement, player selection card, squad position filter, feed category filter, and the debate vote.
- Every video tile and "Watch on TikTok" button opens `https://www.tiktok.com/@ultimatekombo2`.

## Responsive rules

- Design desktop (1280), tablet (820) and mobile (390) frames.
- On mobile: single-column sections, hero portrait under the headline, phase buttons scroll horizontally, squad and feed grids drop to two columns (squad to one column below 520px), and the Barça corner stacks vertically.
- Keep tap targets at least 44px high and text contrast at WCAG AA in both themes.

## Content rules

- All scores, views, votes, video titles and stats are clearly sample content.
- Use placeholders for portraits, photography and club badges. Do not use real player photos, club crests or sponsor logos.
- Use sentence-case, plain-language copy. Buttons say exactly what they do ("Watch on TikTok", "Join the debate").

## Design principles

- One memorable thing: the tactical board. Keep everything around it calm and disciplined.
- Barça is the visual DNA, football is the subject. The site must read as "his football voice", with Barça as one corner of it.
- Editorial and confident, not busy: strong type, generous space, one accent colour (gold) for actions.
